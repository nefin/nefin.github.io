---
title: "Insights"
description: "Essays and tutorials on finance research — guest researchers writing about their own work, and didactic pieces from the NEFIN team, for a wider audience."
---

Insights brings together two kinds of writing: short essays by researchers we invite to
write about their own work in finance, and didactic or informative pieces — tutorials,
walkthroughs, explainers — written by the NEFIN team itself. Each text is signed by its
author. If you would like to contribute, write to [nefin@usp.br](mailto:nefin@usp.br).
